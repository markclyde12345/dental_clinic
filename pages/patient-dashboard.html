Help Center & Patient FAQs

📅 How do I reschedule or cancel an appointment?

Click on the appointment card in your Overview or Book Appointment tab, click "Details", and select Reschedule or Cancel. Please provide at least 24 hours notice.

💳 How does online payment work?

Under Billing & Invoices, click "Pay with PayMongo". You can pay securely with GCash, Maya, GrabPay, or any Philippine Debit/Credit Card. An official receipt is immediately generated.

🦷 Where can I view my treatment history & notes?

Navigate to "My Records" to view your historical treatments, assigned dentists, and branch notes.

📍 What are the clinic hours?

Main Balirong: Mon–Sat 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Minglanilla: Mon–Sun 8:30 AM – 6:30 PM. Emergency dental assistance is available on-call.
