💳 Fano Dental Clinic – Billing & Invoices Submodule Flowchart [ Q ]

Detailed Specification Reference for Draw.io (Billing & Invoices Submodule)

Step Flowchart Symbol Exact Text for draw.io System Functionality in Clinic Codebase
1 Off-page Connector (Pentagon) Q Submodule link branching from Patient Portal ([ BB ]).
2 Rectangle (Process) Billing & Invoices Navigates to #billing-section in patient-dashboard.html.
3 Parallelogram (Data / I/O) Display Bills Renders invoices table (#invoices-table-body) with Treatment, Fee, and Status.
4 Rectangle (Process) Pay Bills Patient clicks "Pay with PayMongo" button (openPaymongoModal()).
5 Parallelogram (Data / I/O) Proceed to Paymongo System calls /api/payments/paymongo/checkout to get hosted session.
6 Parallelogram (Data / I/O) Select Payment Method
(GCash, Maya, Card, GrabPay)		 Patient selects their preferred e-wallet or credit/debit card on checkout screen.
7 Parallelogram (Data / I/O) Input Account Details & OTP Patient enters mobile number, MPIN, and 6-digit OTP verification code.
8 Rectangle (Process) Process & Authorize Payment Gateway validates balance and authenticates transaction with issuing financial provider.
9 Diamond (Decision) Payment Successful? Evaluates whether transaction was successfully authorized or cancelled/declined.
9a Parallelogram (Data / I/O) [No Path] Display Payment Failed / Cancelled Displays error toast; loops back to Display Bills for retry.
10 Rectangle (Process) [Yes Path] Receive Real-time Webhook &
Update Database (is_paid = true)		 PayMongo webhook reconciles invoice and updates database in real-time.
11 Rectangle (Process) Generate Official Receipt Creates digital receipt with unique OR #, breakdown, and timestamp.
12 Parallelogram (Data / I/O) Display Official Receipt &
Download / Print Option		 Opens #modal-payment-receipt modal with Print Receipt button.
13 Rectangle (Process) Send Payment Confirmation
(SMS & Email Voucher)		 Sends confirmation alert to patient's registered phone and email.
14 Rectangle (Process) Return to Overview Returns patient to Dashboard Overview screen.
15 Oval (Terminator) END End of the Billing & Invoices submodule lifecycle.
